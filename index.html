Doctors (modern medicine) per 10,000 people. WHO counts doctors of all ages, so "all ages" is the like-for-like Kerala figure; "under 65" allows for doctors who have probably retired. WHO figures are its density-of-physicians indicator (SDG 3.c.1), for the latest year WHO has published for each place, shown in brackets. They are not projected: WHO gives the world and regional figures for 2024 only, so there is no trend to extend, and at recent rates India would move only from 9.6 to about 9.8 by 2026. Kerala uses a 2026 population of about 36 million (Government of India projection). The Kerala projection counts registrations only: it doesn't subtract doctors who emigrate, leave medicine or die before 65, nor add doctors registered in other states who work in Kerala, so treat it as an upper-end estimate of doctors available.

    How the numbers were made
    • All 67,147 Travancore Cochin Medical Council records were downloaded from the NMC Indian Medical Register, one registration year at a time. One record had its registration number typed in as its year and was recovered separately.
    • Gender is estimated, never recorded. Common first names (covering about three quarters of doctors) were labelled woman, man or unclear with Kerala naming in mind. Rarer names were placed by name endings that point to one gender at least 92% of the time. Everything else is Unknown. Expect a few percent of misreads in both directions.
    • Universities were matched to a state or country by name, including common misspellings. "MD Physician" degrees from Russia, Ukraine and similar countries count as foreign basic degrees.
    • The map places doctors by the PIN code in their permanent address, using India Post's PIN directory; a PIN is kept only where its post offices fall inside the PIN's own district (about 3% of PIN-coded doctors could not be placed this way). District boundaries are the 2011 census districts from DataMeet.
    • Working age is estimated from year of registration (age today ≈ 2026 − registration year + 25), because the register has no active or retired status and few birth dates for older doctors. Doctors per 10,000 people divide these counts by Kerala's projected 2026 population of 36 million, for comparison with WHO's figures. Because Kerala's entries stop in 2019, the 2026 figure adds registrations for 2020–2026 projected from a straight-line fit to 2010–2019.
